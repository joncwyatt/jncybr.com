The trick is patience, twice. Wait until the bananas are mostly black. Then give the butter the extra minute it takes to go from golden to deep brown. Both add a toasty depth that plain melted butter can't.

## Ingredients

### Bread
- 1/2 cup (113g) unsalted butter
- 3 large very ripe bananas (about 340g peeled)
- 2/3 cup (135g) packed light brown sugar
- 2 large eggs, room temperature
- 1/3 cup (80g) sour cream or whole-milk yogurt
- 2 tsp vanilla extract
- 1 3/4 cups (220g) all-purpose flour
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 3/4 tsp kosher salt
- 1 tsp ground cinnamon

### Topping
- 2 tbsp demerara or turbinado sugar
- 1/2 cup (55g) toasted walnuts or pecans, chopped (optional)

## Steps

1. Heat the oven to 350°F (175°C). Grease a 9×5-inch loaf pan and line it with a strip of parchment that hangs over the long sides.
2. Melt the butter in a light-colored saucepan over medium heat, swirling, until the foam subsides and the solids turn deep golden brown and smell nutty, 5–7 minutes. Scrape it into a large bowl, browned bits and all, and cool for 10 minutes.
3. Mash the bananas into the butter until mostly smooth. Whisk in the brown sugar, eggs, sour cream, and vanilla.
4. In a separate bowl, whisk the flour, baking soda, salt, and cinnamon. Fold into the wet ingredients with a spatula until just combined. A few streaks of flour are fine. Fold in the nuts, if using.
5. Scrape the batter into the pan, smooth the top, and sprinkle evenly with the demerara sugar.
6. Bake until a skewer in the center comes out with a few moist crumbs, 55–65 minutes. If the top darkens too fast, tent it loosely with foil after 40 minutes.
7. Cool in the pan for 15 minutes, then lift out by the parchment and cool on a rack before slicing.

## Notes

- **Bananas:** the darker the better. In a pinch, roast unpeeled bananas at 300°F for 15–20 minutes until black, then cool.
- **Storage:** keeps wrapped at room temperature for 3 days. Or freeze individual slices for up to 3 months and toast them from frozen.
