I make a batch of these on Sunday and pack them for the week. Store the dressing and chickpeas separately until you eat, so everything stays crisp.

## Ingredients

### Farro
- 1 cup (200g) farro, rinsed
- 1 tsp kosher salt

### Crispy chickpeas
- 2 (15-oz) cans chickpeas, drained, rinsed, and patted very dry
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1/2 tsp ground cumin
- 1/2 tsp kosher salt

### Lemon-tahini dressing
- 1/3 cup (80g) tahini
- 1/4 cup (60ml) fresh lemon juice
- 1 small garlic clove, finely grated
- 3-5 tbsp cold water
- 1/2 tsp kosher salt

### To serve
- 1 English cucumber, diced
- 1 cup (150g) cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1/2 small red onion, thinly sliced
- 1 cup mixed soft herbs (parsley, dill, mint)
- 4 oz (115g) feta, crumbled

## Steps

1. Heat the oven to 425°F (220°C).
2. Bring a medium pot of water to a boil. Add the farro and salt and simmer until tender but still chewy, 25–30 minutes. Drain well and spread on a plate to cool.
3. Meanwhile, toss the chickpeas with the olive oil, paprika, cumin, and salt on a rimmed baking sheet. Roast, shaking the pan halfway through, until deeply golden and crisp, 25–30 minutes.
4. Whisk the tahini, lemon juice, garlic, and salt. It will seize and thicken. Whisk in cold water a tablespoon at a time until smooth and pourable.
5. Divide the farro among bowls and top with the cucumber, tomatoes, onion, herbs, feta, and chickpeas. Drizzle with dressing just before eating.

## Notes

- **Meal prep:** store the farro and vegetables together, and the chickpeas and dressing separately. Re-crisp the chickpeas in a 400°F oven or air fryer for about 4 minutes.
- Swap the farro for quinoa, brown rice, or pearl couscous.
