Chopped bar chocolate is the whole point here. It melts into thin layers and puddles in a way chips never do. Keep the shards and dust when you chop.

## Ingredients

### Dough
- 1 cup (227g) unsalted butter, softened
- 3/4 cup (150g) packed dark brown sugar
- 1/2 cup (100g) granulated sugar
- 1 large egg, room temperature
- 1 large egg yolk
- 2 tsp vanilla extract
- 2 1/4 cups (280g) all-purpose flour
- 3/4 tsp baking soda
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- 8 oz (225g) dark chocolate (60–70%), roughly chopped

### To finish
- Flaky sea salt

## Steps

1. Beat the butter and both sugars with a stand or hand mixer on medium speed until creamy and lighter in color, about 2 minutes. Beat in the egg, yolk, and vanilla until smooth.
2. Whisk the flour, baking soda, and salt in a separate bowl. Add to the butter mixture and mix on low just until no dry streaks remain. Stir in the chocolate.
3. Portion into 18 balls (about 55g each), set them on a plate, cover, and refrigerate for at least 2 hours or up to 3 days.
4. Heat the oven to 350°F (175°C) and line two baking sheets with parchment. Arrange the dough balls at least 3 inches apart, 6 to 8 per sheet.
5. Bake one sheet at a time until the edges are golden but the centers still look underdone, 11–13 minutes. Sprinkle with flaky salt right away.
6. Cool on the sheet for 5 minutes, then move to a rack.

## Notes

- The rest isn't strictly required, but it hydrates the flour and deepens the flavor. Overnight is the sweet spot.
- Freeze portioned dough for up to 3 months. Bake from frozen, adding 1–2 minutes.
