My default weeknight dinner. The potatoes roast in the chicken fat underneath, and the garlic goes in late so it turns sweet and jammy instead of burnt.

## Ingredients

### Chicken and potatoes
- 6 bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs (about 1.1kg)
- 2 tsp kosher salt, divided
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 1 1/2 lb (680g) baby Yukon Gold potatoes, halved
- 1 medium red onion, cut into wedges
- 3 tbsp olive oil, divided
- 1 tsp dried oregano

### Lemon garlic finish
- 1 lemon, thinly sliced, seeds removed
- 1 head garlic, cloves separated and peeled
- 2 tbsp fresh lemon juice
- 1 tbsp honey
- 2 tbsp chopped parsley

## Steps

1. Pat the chicken dry and season it all over with 1½ tsp of the salt and the pepper. If you have time, leave it uncovered in the fridge for up to a day. The skin crisps even better.
2. Heat the oven to 425°F (220°C) with a rack in the upper-middle position.
3. On a rimmed baking sheet, toss the potatoes and onion with 2 tbsp of the oil, the oregano, and the remaining ½ tsp salt. Spread out, cut sides down.
4. Nestle the chicken skin-side up among the vegetables and rub the skin with the remaining oil.
5. Roast for 25 minutes. Scatter the lemon slices and garlic around the chicken, tossing them in the pan juices. Roast until the skin is deeply browned and the thighs reach 175°F (80°C), 15–20 minutes more.
6. Whisk the lemon juice and honey, spoon it over the chicken and potatoes, and finish with parsley. Serve straight from the pan.

## Notes

- Thighs are forgiving. They stay juicy anywhere from 175°F to 195°F, so go for more color.
- Boneless thighs work too. Cut the total roasting time to about 30 minutes.
