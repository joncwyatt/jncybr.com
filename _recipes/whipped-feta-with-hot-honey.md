What I make when people are coming over and I haven't planned anything. It looks like more effort than it is.

## Ingredients

### Whipped feta
- 8 oz (225g) block feta, drained and crumbled
- 4 oz (115g) cream cheese, room temperature
- 2 tbsp olive oil, plus more to finish
- 1 tsp lemon zest
- 1 tbsp fresh lemon juice

### Hot honey
- 3 tbsp honey
- 1-2 tsp Calabrian chili paste (or red pepper flakes, to taste)
- 1 tsp apple cider vinegar
- Flaky salt and black pepper

### To serve
- Warm pita, crackers, cucumbers, and radishes

## Steps

1. Blend the feta and cream cheese in a food processor until mostly smooth, about 1 minute. With the motor running, stream in the olive oil, lemon zest, and lemon juice. Blend until light and whipped, 1–2 minutes more, scraping down the sides as needed.
2. Warm the honey, chili, and vinegar in a small saucepan, or in the microwave in 15-second bursts, until fluid. Let sit 5 minutes to infuse.
3. Spread the feta into a shallow bowl, swooping with the back of a spoon to make wells. Drizzle with the hot honey and a little more olive oil, then finish with flaky salt and pepper.

## Notes

- Keeps covered in the fridge for up to 4 days. Let it sit at room temperature for 20 minutes before serving, and add the honey just before.
- Feta sold in brine has the best texture. Pre-crumbled feta is drier and won't whip as smoothly.
