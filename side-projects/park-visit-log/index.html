Park Visit Log

Visit · Log · Remember

A personal tracker for the national parks of the United States and Canada: all 64 US parks and 40 in Canada. Log every visit with photos, notes, and what you saw, watch your progress grow on a live map, earn badges, and relive your park memories. Every park has a hand-drawn landmark tile, and each park you visit earns a dated passport stamp. Built to be fun and lightweight — no account required, no tracking, all data stays on your device.

Highlights

Features

  • ✓ Track visits to all 104 national parks in the US and Canada, with photos, notes, and wildlife sightings
  • ✓ Interactive map and GPS check-in against official park boundaries
  • ✓ Closest Park card with a live compass, plus Parks Near Me
  • ✓ A wishlist, Collections like Utah’s Mighty 5, and a Badge Book of milestones, seasons, streaks, and regions
  • ✓ Field Passport of stamps, Year in Review, yearly goals, and shareable progress cards
  • ✓ Live NPS data for US parks: alerts, hours, fees, and official photos
  • ✓ Sun & Sky: sunrise, sunset, golden hour, and moon phase for every park
  • ✓ Home and Lock Screen widgets, Siri, and Spotlight
  • ✓ Made for iPhone Duo: unfold it and the app opens like an atlas, map on one page and park on the other
  • ✓ 100% on-device. No account, no server, no tracking.
